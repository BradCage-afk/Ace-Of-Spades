SAATHI - TECH TALK NOTES (Team Ace Of Spades, 27 Nov 2026, 10:20 AM)
Say each part in about 15 seconds. Show it in the demo where you can.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE SHAPE (slide 5)
------------------------------------------------------------
"Saathi has three parts: a Chrome extension in your browser, a small
proxy that holds our API key, and an AI on NVIDIA's cloud. Everything
that touches your data runs on your computer."

  - Extension: reads the page's labels and draws the cues.
  - Proxy: keeps the API key out of the extension; switches to a
    backup model if the main one is slow or busy.
  - AI: MiniMax M3.1 Flash on NVIDIA NIM, with Nemotron as backup.
    It only decides WHERE to point.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. ON-DEVICE VOICE
------------------------------------------------------------
"Voice is turned into text in the browser with Whisper, so your audio
is never uploaded. Spoken replies use only voices installed on your
computer."

  - The voice model is downloaded once, then runs locally.
  - You see the transcript and can fix it before sending.
  - Typing always works.
  - HONEST: Hindi voice input isn't reliable yet (our test: the
    standard model was unusable; a Hindi-tuned one cut errors from
    297% to 16%, but it is a 400 MB download). Kannada voice is not
    offered yet. Typed Hindi goals work.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRIVACY RECEIPT
------------------------------------------------------------
"Only the page's labels and your goal are sent, never what you type
into fields. Numbers and codes are masked first. The privacy receipt
shows the exact text sent, so you can check instead of trusting us."

  - Form pages are planned on your laptop, so nothing is sent at all.
  - DEMO: open "Privacy receipt" in the panel after one step.
  - HONEST: masking catches phone numbers, emails, PAN and OTP-like
    codes, not names or addresses.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. UNDER THE HOOD
------------------------------------------------------------
"Under the hood it's Chrome Manifest V3 with a background worker, so it
keeps guiding even when you close the panel, plain JavaScript, and
Node.js for the proxy. No server database and no user accounts."

  - Point-only: the AI can return just five cues (tap, type, select,
    scroll, pause). A local check drops anything not on the page.
  - Passwords, OTP, CAPTCHA, payment and final submit always pause.
  - Forms are planned on the device, so form pages are instant.
  - Steps for a repeated page are remembered for 24 hours.
  - HONEST: runs locally today; AWS hosting is planned.

------------------------------------------------------------
POSSIBLE TECHNICAL QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why not run the AI on the device?
A: Chrome's built-in AI doesn't support Indian languages yet and needs
   a powerful machine. So the AI runs in the cloud but only sees page
   labels; everything personal stays local.

Q: Why MiniMax?
A: In our speed test it answered fastest on NVIDIA NIM, and it
   understood typed Hindi goals. Nemotron is the backup.

Q: What if the AI is slow or down?
A: After 25 seconds we also ask the backup and use whichever answers
   first. If both fail, Saathi points at the best keyword match and
   says so. Forms don't need the AI at all.

Q: How do you stop prompt injection?
A: By limiting what the AI can do, not by trusting it. Page text is
   treated as untrusted, the AI can only point, and sensitive steps
   always pause. Our mock site has a fake "pay now" trick link; Saathi
   ignored it in our test.

Q: Is it really an agent if it doesn't click?
A: Yes. It understands the goal, reads the page, plans, watches what
   you do and replans. We leave the click to you on purpose.

Q: What if it points at the wrong thing?
A: You can ignore it, press "I'm stuck", or Start over. A wrong point
   costs a moment; a wrong click could cost money.

Q: How does it know you did the step?
A: It listens for your real click or a filled field on the page, then
   moves on. It only checks that a field is filled, not what is in it.

Q: Does it work on any website?
A: It reads any page's labels with no per-site setup. Tested on our
   mock portal (5 tasks) and public pages of scholarships.gov.in, not
   logged-in forms. Embedded frames and image-only buttons are a gap.

Q: Why Hindi steps from templates?
A: Writing long Hindi answers made the AI slow. Now the AI just picks
   the item and Saathi writes the Hindi sentence, which is instant.

Q: Mobile?
A: Desktop Chrome for now; Chrome on Android generally doesn't run
   extensions. Cyber cafes and service centres use desktops.

Q: Is it open source?
A: Yes: github.com/BradCage-afk/Ace-Of-Spades
